interviewer.glb is a custom export of the MakeHuman / MPFB assets packaged by
HumanForge v0.5.0 (CC0 1.0). It includes the culturalibre_hair_02 hairstyle by
culturalibre (CC0 1.0) and the MakeHuman male_casualsuit01 outfit (CC0 1.0).
Asset sources: https://github.com/abdulrehman043/humanforge/blob/b3e5885ade1b3d48e12994a121e33e07e806390b/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

interviewer-v2.glb is a new custom export of the same CC0 MakeHuman / MPFB
asset package (HumanForge v0.5.0, revision above). It uses the short02 hair,
male_elegantsuit01 outfit, brown eyes, and 34 non-zero facial expression
targets. Standard glTF sparse accessors preserve the original vertex data
without an external decoder. The optical frames are generated by this
project. No Ready Player Me or other non-commercial avatar assets are used.

model (face_landmarker.task) are Copyright The MediaPipe Authors, licensed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0. You may obtain a copy of the License at
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Model source: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task
Camera frames are processed on-device only and are never uploaded.
